GraySite Privacy Policy

Published 2026-11-25 · Effective 2026-11-25

Team Light (representative: PARK KWANG HUN, "Operator") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws, and processes the personal information of users of GraySite ("Game") as described below.

This is an English translation. If it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

1. Information we process and why

CategoryItemsPurposeHow collected
AccountStore (Steam or STOVE) account identifier, nicknameSign-in verification, matchmaking, showing players in a matchReceived automatically from the store platform when the Game starts
ConnectionIP address, connection time, matchmaking and connection logsConnecting to game servers, troubleshooting, preventing abuseGenerated automatically when matchmaking or connecting to a server
  • Voice chat is relayed in real time to teammates only and is not stored.
  • Game settings, replays and profile customization are stored only on your PC and are not collected by the Operator.
  • The Operator does not directly collect your name, contact details or payment information. (The Game is free.)

2. Retention and deletion

  1. Connection and matchmaking logs are kept for 30 days from creation and then deleted automatically.
  2. Where other laws require retention, information is kept for the required period.
  3. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

3. Processors

ProcessorTaskLocation

4. Sharing with third parties and international transfer

  1. The Operator does not provide your personal information to third parties, except when required by law.
  2. To verify sign-in, the Game sends an authentication request to the servers of the store platform you use.
    • Steam: Valve Corporation (United States) — Steam sign-in verification
    • STOVE: Smilegate, Inc. (Republic of Korea) — STOVE sign-in verification
    • Each platform processes your information under its own privacy policy.

5. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information at any time. Contact us at the address below and we will act without delay.

6. Children

The Game is offered according to the age rating it receives. The Operator does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

7. Security measures

  • Server access is limited to operations staff, and access is logged.
  • Matchmaking traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Secrets such as authentication keys are kept in a separate secure store.
